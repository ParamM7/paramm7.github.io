ACTIVE RESEARCH

Thermally-coupled multiphase electrohydrodynamics

Extending interEHDFoam to couple the sensible-enthalpy transport equation with multiphase EHD flow — enabling thermally-coupled simulations of Taylor bubble dynamics under external electric fields in confined capillary geometries.

KEY CONTRIBUTIONS
  • Finite-volume discretisation of sensible-enthalpy equation with convective + diffusive flux assembly
  • Integration with PISO pressure–velocity–electric-field iterative loops; Co ≤ 0.5 stability
  • Investigating EHD body-force–driven interfacial instabilities and bubble morphology
  • Benchmarking against canonical EHD thermal datasets · under Prof. Absar Lakdawala

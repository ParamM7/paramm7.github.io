Mechanical Engineering Undergraduate

Third-year undergraduate at Nirma University with a focus on CFD solver development, multiphase flow physics, and autonomous UAV systems.

Research trajectory

My academic focus spans computational fluid dynamics, numerical methods, and electrohydrodynamics. Under Prof. Absar Lakdawala, I am extending the OpenFOAM-based interEHDFoam solver to incorporate the energy transport equation — enabling thermally-coupled multiphase electrohydrodynamic simulations. This work involves finite-volume discretisation of the sensible enthalpy equation, interfacial instability analysis, and Taylor flow in capillary channels under external electric fields.

Engineering practice

As Analysis & Technical Lead at Team Arrow, Nirma University's competitive UAV team, I led computational aerodynamic analysis for multi-rotor and fixed-wing platforms. The team claimed first place globally at the Student Unmanned Aerial Systems (SUAS) competition 2025 in Maryland, USA, competing against 81 international teams.

My technical interests span CFD solver development, multiphase flow physics, autonomous UAV systems, and the application of high-performance numerical methods to engineering problems.

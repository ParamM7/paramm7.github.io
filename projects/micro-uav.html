Micro-Class Fixed-Wing UAV
— design evolution & CFD validation.

Aerodynamic performance assessment of a micro-class fixed-wing UAV — XFLR5 panel methods cross-validated with RANS simulations. Published at FMFP 2025.

Context

Micro-class UAVs (wingspan < 1 m) operate at low Reynolds numbers where viscous effects dominate, separation is common, and airfoil selection carries outsized influence on L/D ratio, stall margin, and stability. Designing them well demands both panel-method speed and RANS accuracy.

Approach

  • Initial geometry iteration and airfoil down-selection in XFLR5: panel / VLM analysis, stability derivatives, and polar curves across operating Re.
  • High-fidelity validation in ANSYS Fluent: RANS with k–ω SST turbulence closure, y⁺ ≈ 1 mesh, lift/drag polars, and pressure-coefficient distributions at design and off-design angles of attack.
  • Cross-comparison of XFLR5 and RANS results — establishing agreement in the linear regime and divergence at the onset of separation.
  • Stability & control: static and dynamic margins verified against required flight envelope.

Publication

Design Evolution and Aerodynamic Performance Assessment of a Micro-Class Fixed-Wing Unmanned Aerial Vehicle — 12th International & 52nd National Conference on Fluid Mechanics and Fluid Power (FMFP 2025), Nirma University, Ahmedabad. Accepted, presented, and published in the conference proceedings.

Outcomes

  • Finalised airframe geometry delivered to the build team with documented aerodynamic performance.
  • Peer-reviewed publication establishing the XFLR5 ↔ RANS cross-validation workflow for undergraduate UAV design pipelines.
  • Workflow templated and re-used on a follow-up high-payload UAV study (manuscript in preparation for Journal of Flow Visualization and Image Processing).

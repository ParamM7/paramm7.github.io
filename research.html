Thread 01

Multiphase Electrohydrodynamics Solver

Extending the interEHDFoam solver (EHD variant of interFoam) to couple the energy transport equation with multiphase EHD flow — enabling thermally-coupled electrohydrodynamic simulations. Finite-volume discretisation of the sensible-enthalpy transport equation with diffusive and convective flux assembly. Investigating EHD body-force-driven interfacial instabilities and bubble-morphology deformation under external electric fields in confined capillary geometries.

Thread 02

Taylor Flow in Capillaries Under Electric Fields

Simulating Taylor bubble flow dynamics in capillary channels subject to external electric fields in OpenFOAM. Resolving interfacial deformation, bubble elongation, and velocity-field redistribution under EHD forcing. Studying coupling between Maxwell-stress-induced body forces and multiphase flow topology in confined, low-Bond-number geometries. Courant-number stability (Co ≤ 0.5) and iterative solver convergence for coupled pressure–velocity–electric field solutions.

Thread 03

UAV Aerodynamic Analysis & Validation

Full-configuration CFD of fixed-wing and hybrid VTOL platforms using ANSYS Fluent RANS with rotating MRF subdomain at approximately 9 390 RPM. Analysis of propeller-wake and lifting-surface interaction during VTOL-to-fixed-wing transition; quantification of induced drag and pitching-moment perturbations. Pressure-coefficient distributions, lift/drag polars, and wake-interaction studies to guide geometry refinement. Validated aerodynamic performance of micro-class fixed-wing UAV using XFLR5 panel-method analysis alongside RANS simulations.

Undergraduate Research

Undergraduate Researcher

Multiphase Flow Research Lab · Nirma University
Aug 2025 — Present

Working under Prof. Absar Lakdawala on solver-level extension of multiphase electrohydrodynamic CFD. Current scope: incorporating the energy-transport equation into the interEHDFoam solver (OpenFOAM) and running Taylor-flow simulations in capillary channels with external electric fields.

  • Extending interEHDFoam — an EHD variant of interFoam — to couple the energy transport equation with multiphase EHD flow simulations.
  • Implementing finite-volume discretisation of the sensible enthalpy transport equation, including diffusive and convective flux assembly.
  • Running OpenFOAM simulations of Taylor-flow in capillary channels with external electric fields; resolving interfacial dynamics, bubble deformation, and velocity-field redistribution.
  • Verifying Courant-number stability (Co ≤ 0.5) and iterative solver convergence for coupled pressure–velocity–electric field solutions.
